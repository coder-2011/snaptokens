`b3e8a34415f51a4ba4cb9b05d65bccfbd9ed8cfa` and evaluator
`d9086dffdb6b197e47156c32de5beb16ac89c0d9c90c92297d94d7a9b661763b`.

score, the published summary, the failure inventory, and a lossless archive of
the raw fresh-process JSONL files. Detailed interpretation and commands are in
`log.md`; the active gates are in `autoresearch/state.md`.
